Star Regions

Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · Published by Steven Thompson, United States

Star Regions is a logic puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. Nothing you do in it leaves your device: there is no account, no analytics, no tracking, and the app itself never connects to the internet.

In short

  • Star Regions does not collect personal data. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • The only two links in the app — to this page and to the support page — open in your web browser. The app itself makes no network connections.
  • There are no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or social-media SDKs, and the app does not track you across apps or websites.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Your settings: appearance, haptics, the assists you have switched on or off (Auto-X, conflict highlighting, timer display), bold region outlines, the board size you last played, and which one-time tips you have already seen.
  • The puzzle you are playing: the board, your marks and stars, the move history used by Undo, the time on the clock, and how many hints and restarts you have used on it.
  • Your progress per board size: puzzles solved, points and best times.
  • The order of puzzles: a random number created the first time you open the app, and one position counter per board size, used only to deal puzzles in an order of their own on your device so you do not meet the same puzzle again. The number is not derived from your device or from you and is never sent anywhere.

We cannot see any of this, and we have no way to link it to you.

Clearing it. Settings → Reset progress clears solved counts, points and best times. Deleting the app removes everything listed above.

Backups. Like other app data, this information may be included in your iCloud or computer backups if device backups are turned on. Those backups are managed by you and Apple; we have no access to them.

Permissions

Star Regions does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, calendars, health data or notifications, and it does not ask for permission to track you.

Third parties

There are no third-party SDKs in the app. We do not sell, rent, share or trade personal information — we do not have any to begin with.

Information Apple may provide to us

Apple gives app publishers aggregated statistics, such as the number of downloads. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may also give us crash reports and usage statistics. Apple collects this under its own privacy policy and passes it to us in a form that does not identify you. We use it only to find and fix problems, and you can turn the sharing off in the same iOS setting at any time.

If you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to answer you, do not add you to any mailing list, and delete the correspondence once the question is resolved and it is no longer needed.

Children

Star Regions is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 or under the minimum age that applies in your country. It has no chat, no user-generated content and no purchases.

Your rights

Laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights over personal data a business holds about you: to access, correct, delete or export it, to object to its use, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. Because the app collects no personal data, we hold nothing about you from the app to access, correct, export or delete, and nothing is sold or shared. The information on your device stays under your control: reset it in Settings or delete the app.

If you have emailed us, you can ask us at any time to delete that correspondence, and we will.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. If a future version were ever to collect any data, we would say so here and in the app’s privacy details on the App Store before that version is released.
